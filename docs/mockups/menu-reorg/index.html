What changes

  • Seven tabs become five in 2D: Field, Draw, Colour, Fluid, Matrix. Arrows and Flow merge into Draw, which starts with one choice: arrows, particles, streamlines, or arrows with particles. Curve was a whole tab for one checkbox; it moves into Field.
  • 3D shows three tabs. Fluid and Matrix only work on the 2D calculator. Today they still appear in 3D with all their controls, and do nothing.
  • Said once, not seven times. The field picker and Generate / Remove / Reset sit outside the tabs. In Before they are outlined in red: they are repeated on every tab.
  • Fine-tuning folds away. Each tab shows what most people change. Everything else stays one click away under More, still with the same defaults.
  • 31 palettes become one grouped list: Scientific, Diverging, Cyclic, Majestic. Today they are 31 buttons in a row.
