# What to build next for AP Physics 1 and 2

Build the six demos ranked first below: **projectile motion, oscillator phase space, circular motion, continuity/Bernoulli, a conductor in uniform E, and Faraday induction**. They address common reasoning errors using capabilities already in the branch. This package implements those six; the other 22 rows are a prioritized backlog, not claims of completed demos.

Base commit: `d2217295628de72c412a3b0a9fe1013492a821c7`, which contains the requested `71a48d97` and the new Majestic/Explanatory mode. No push. `implementation.patch` and `source/` contain the implementation; `designs.md` gives the full six designs and exact interpretation; `preset-configurations.json` has every emitted formula, variable, switch and scene. Start with `verification.md` for measured results and limitations.

## Ranking

**Value is my classroom-design judgment, not a teacher survey or measured learning gain.** Order weighs misconception clarity, usefulness in a short live explanation, curriculum reach, implementation cost, and whether the picture's meaning remains honest. Small means formulas/scenes using existing mechanisms; Medium means more coordination and validation; a named capability means reusable engine work. A fixed closed-form demonstration may fit today even when a general editable tool does not.

## Where each plugin fits

**Vector Tools** owns the visual demonstrations, with analytical fields, native geometry, probes and clocks. The six new presets use `GalleryPreset`, `SceneItem.explains`, `probeArrows2D/3D`, `sceneTime`, `tint`, `meaning` and the real mode switch. No imaginary circuit, ray or wave API was added. Their Majestic views suppress explanatory objects and axis text; Explanatory restores the teacher's prior axes/grid settings.

**Fluid tab:** the code uses CPU/GPU D2Q9 lattice Boltzmann with explicit lattice units and momentum-exchange force measurements (`src/field-rendering/sim/lbm/d2q9.ts`, `GpuD2Q9.ts`, `links.ts`). It is useful for comparing viscous flow and body drag with the ideal streamtube demo. It is not automatically a calibrated thermodynamics, free-surface buoyancy, or general acoustic simulator. The analytical nozzle is not claimed to have been simulated in Fluid tab.

**Audio Lab:** inspection of `AudioLabSession.ts` and `AudioLabRuntime.ts` found audio input/playback feeding an analyser and field displays. A media source can play through the analyser to the audio destination. I did **not** find a phase-coherent physics-tone generator or a general field-to-sound synthesizer. Thus it can compare supplied tones, spectra and microphone signals for the wave demos now; controlled synthesis belongs to capability C4 below. A dominant-frequency sine is not the original waveform.

**Physics Lab** remains a neighbor: use it for differential equations, phase-plane reasoning, work/energy integrals, symbolic rearrangement and derivations. Do not put a new general circuit solver or optical bench into Vector Tools merely because their results can be drawn there.

## Boundaries that matter in class

- A flow particle can visualize a vector field without representing a physical object obeying that field as a force. Every new legend/descriptive caption names the field. Exact clocked markers carry the calibrated motion demonstrations.
- The 3D oscillator is intentionally a view of a two-coordinate phase plane, not a fictitious three-dimensional oscillator. A small seed thickness is a display tolerance only.
- The conductor is a sphere section in 2D. The nozzle is an exact ideal streamtube, not a general real nozzle. Induction is the quasistatic prescribed-solenoid model, not a radiation solution.
- Keep AP1 rotation centered on the course's clockwise/counterclockwise and scalar angular-momentum treatment. A 3D camera is a visualization aid, not a claim that angular-momentum vector directions are AP1 exam content (CED boundary under 6.3).
- No text or vector names is drawn by these presets in Majestic; meaning stays available in the presets panel. Vector names are optional in Explanatory. A later “Majestic plus names” override, if desired, must be explicit rather than violating this brief's no-writing mode.
